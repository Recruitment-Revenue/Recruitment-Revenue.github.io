1. Introduction

RecRev Ltd (“we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard personal information when you interact with our website, forms, or services.

By using our website, submitting your details, or engaging with our services, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Contact Information: name, company, job title, email address, phone number, LinkedIn profile.
  • Business Information: company details, hiring activity, and recruitment interests.
  • Technical Data: IP address, browser type, operating system, and website analytics.
  • Communication Data: messages, call recordings, or SMS replies related to your engagement with us.

3. How We Collect Information

We collect data when you:

  • Complete contact or onboarding forms.
  • Book meetings or calls with our team.
  • Communicate with us via email, phone, or SMS.
  • Interact with our website or marketing campaigns.

4. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Respond to enquiries and deliver services.
  • Communicate about meetings, campaigns, and updates.
  • Send SMS or email reminders relating to your service.
  • Improve our website, outreach, and client experience.
  • Comply with legal and regulatory obligations.

5. Legal Basis for Processing (UK GDPR)

We process personal data based on:

  • Legitimate Interest: contacting and servicing B2B clients.
  • Contractual Necessity: fulfilling client deliverables.
  • Consent: for marketing or optional communications.

6. Data Sharing

We share personal data only with trusted partners who assist in delivering our services, such as CRM, communication, and analytics tools (e.g., Close, Smartlead, Clay, Google Workspace). We never sell or rent your personal data.

7. International Data Transfers

Some providers process data outside the UK (e.g., the US). We use Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) and equivalent safeguards to ensure adequate protection.

8. Data Retention

We keep personal data only as long as necessary — typically up to 24 months for marketing data, or longer if legally required.

9. Your Rights (UK & EU)

You have the right to:

  • Access, correct, or delete your data.
  • Object to or restrict processing.
  • Request data portability or withdraw consent.

To exercise your rights, email privacy@recrev.com. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

10. Your Rights (US Residents)

Depending on state law (e.g., California, Virginia, Colorado), you may have rights to:

  • Access or delete personal data.
  • Request correction.
  • Opt out of data sharing (we do not sell personal data).

Requests can be sent to privacy@recrev.com.

11. Cookies

We use cookies and similar technologies to improve functionality and analytics. You can manage cookie preferences in your browser settings.

12. SMS and Email Communications

By providing your phone number or email, you consent to receive service-related messages. You may opt out anytime by replying STOP to SMS or using the unsubscribe link in emails.

13. Security

We maintain technical and organisational safeguards to prevent unauthorised access, loss, or misuse of personal data.

14. Updates

We may update this policy periodically. Any changes will appear on this page with a new Effective Date.

15. Contact Us

For privacy-related questions, contact: privacy@recrev.com
