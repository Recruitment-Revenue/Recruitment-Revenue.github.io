1. Introduction
Recruitment Revenue Ltd (“we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data.
This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard personal information when you interact with our website, forms, or services.
By using our website, submitting your details, or engaging with our services, you agree to this Privacy Policy.
2. Information We Collect
We may collect the following types of information:
Contact Information: name, company, job title, email address, phone number, LinkedIn profile.
Business Information: company details, hiring activity, and recruitment interests.
Technical Data: IP address, browser type, operating system, and website analytics.
Communication Data: messages, call recordings, or SMS replies related to your engagement with us.
3. How We Collect Information
We collect data when you:
Complete contact or onboarding forms.
Book meetings or calls with our team.
Communicate with us via email, phone, or SMS.
Interact with our website or marketing campaigns.
4. How We Use Your Information
We use your information to:
Respond to enquiries and deliver services.
Communicate about meetings, campaigns, and updates.
Send SMS or email reminders relating to your service.
Improve our website, outreach, and client experience.
Comply with legal and regulatory obligations.
5. Legal Basis for Processing (UK GDPR)
We process personal data based on:
Legitimate Interest: contacting and servicing B2B clients.
Contractual Necessity: fulfilling client deliverables.
Consent: for marketing or optional communications.
6. Data Sharing
We share personal data only with trusted partners who assist in delivering our services, such as:
CRM, communication, and analytics tools (e.g., Close, Smartlead, Clay, Google Workspace).
We never sell or rent your personal data.
7. International Data Transfers
Some providers process data outside the UK (e.g., the US).
We use Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) and equivalent safeguards to ensure adequate protection.
8. Data Retention
We keep personal data only as long as necessary — typically up to 24 months for marketing data, or longer if legally required.

Access, correct, or delete your data.
