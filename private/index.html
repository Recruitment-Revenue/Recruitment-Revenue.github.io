01How do you stop emails from landing in spam?+
We use advanced techniques to ensure our emails reach your inbox. This includes email authentication protocols like SPF, DKIM, and DMARC, trusted email servers, and personalised, high-quality content. We also regularly update our email lists and include easy unsubscribe options to maintain a positive sender reputation.
02How do you book the meetings into my calendar?+
We connect your calendar to RecDash Bookings. When someone books a meeting, it automatically appears in your calendar at an available time, saving you the hassle of manual scheduling.
03What if the lead isn't qualified?+
These leads are typically qualified as we get them approved by you. We personalise our emails deeply so the prospects feel like we know their problems. We even handle any objections they bring over email to improve qualification. This is how we convert leads into meeting-ready leads.
