Common Questions

How do you stop emails from landing in spam?

We use advanced techniques to ensure our emails reach your inbox. This includes email authentication protocols like SPF, DKIM, and DMARC, trusted email servers, and personalized, high-quality content. We also regularly update our email lists and include easy unsubscribe options to maintain a positive sender reputation. These measures help us avoid spam filters and ensure our emails are delivered to your inbox.

How do you book the meetings into my calendar?

We set up Calendly for you, which integrates with your calendar. When someone books a meeting, it automatically appears in your calendar at an available time, saving you the hassle of manual scheduling.

What if the lead isn't qualified?

These leads are typically qualified as we get them approved by you. We personalize our emails deeply so the prospects feel like we know their problems. We even handle any objections they bring about over email to improve qualification. This is how we convert leads into meeting-ready-leads!

How much does it cost?

Our payment structure is entirely on performance, based on the specific needs of each client, please book a call to find out more.
