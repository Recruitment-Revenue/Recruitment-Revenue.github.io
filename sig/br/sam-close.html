  1. Press Copy the code above.
  2. In Close: Settings → Email → Email Signature. Click the <> button to switch the box to code view.
  3. Clear whatever is in the box, paste, then click <> again to come back to the normal view. You should see the signature exactly as above.
  4. Click above the picture and type your sign-off (for example Kind regards,), so the picture sits under your name.
  5. Save.
