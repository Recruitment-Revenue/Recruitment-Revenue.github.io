Frequently Asked Questions

01What exactly do you do?+
We book recruitment agencies qualified sales meetings with CEOs, founders, and MDs at companies with a confirmed hiring need and willingness to use an agency, straight into your calendar, no effort from your team. You get a dedicated caller trained in your sector, making 200+ dials a day with a 90-minute talk-time minimum. We sell the meeting; you sell your service.
02Who is this for?+
Best fit: agencies strong on delivery but inconsistent on BD, where new business slips whenever things get busy, or where the owner is the only one doing outreach. Not a fit if you've traded under 12 months, lack delivery capacity right now, or your addressable market is under 1,000 companies.
03What information do I get before a meeting?+
A full brief ahead of every meeting: their agency history, hiring cycles, and current and upcoming roles, so you're never walking in cold.
04How long before I see results?+
After onboarding, most clients see their first meeting within a week; our fastest was 4 hours. By day 90, you're guaranteed at least 15 qualified meetings, with most clients well above that.
05How are you different?+
We work exclusively with recruitment agencies; every script and process is built around this one industry. With 5M+ recruitment-specific outbound dials, real human callers (no AI or automation), and a deep understanding of what makes a meeting actually convert for recruiters, we go far beyond "getting a meeting booked".
06What does 'qualified meeting' mean?+
Every meeting must pass three checks: it's with a real decision maker, they have a genuine hiring need, and they've confirmed they're open to using a recruitment agency. We also align on fees during onboarding. No-shows are rebooked free.
07What is the worst case scenario?+
If we don't deliver 15 qualified meetings in 90 days, you get a full refund, no arguments. With 5M+ dials across hundreds of agencies, falling short is rare.
08We can't risk our brand reputation.+
Your reputation matters most. Onboarding covers your niche, ICP, and no-go contacts, and outreach only goes live once you've approved the script. You'll have direct access to your SDR and campaign manager throughout.
09What if you share leads amongst clients?+
We never do. Every lead is exclusive to you. Our business depends on every client seeing ROI, so sharing leads would work against us.
